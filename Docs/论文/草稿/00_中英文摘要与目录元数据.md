## 中文摘要

为了改善传统联机第一人称射击游戏后期战局节奏趋于固化的问题，基于Unity 2022.3.62f3c1引擎、通用渲染管线URP与Photon PUN 2联机框架，设计并实现了多人联机游戏《共鸣之境》。系统由底层调度与Addressables配置管理、Photon混合通信、第一人称与八类武器战斗、共鸣动态环境联动及AI机器人五大模块构成。客户端通过 `UpdateManager` 集中帧调度与 `ObjectRecycler` 对象池降低运行时开销，依托 `IPunObservable` 插值平滑、RPC与房间属性同步角色位姿与战局事件，并由房主权威结算全局共鸣值以联动触发重力调制、地面火海、中立AI增援、空投补给与陨石打击五类动态环境响应。经从主菜单接入Photon云端房间实测，共鸣数值与五类环境响应全部准确同步，激活46处火海与AI并发交火下平均帧率达67.7 FPS，Gen0垃圾回收增量为0，验证了系统架构的稳定性与可玩性。

【关键词】Unity；第一人称射击；Photon PUN；通用渲染管线；共鸣机制

## 末页英文标题与摘要

Design and Implementation of the Multiplayer Online Game "The Realm of Resonance" Based on Unity

Concord University College Fujian Normal University
Department of Information Technology  Digital Media Technology
124232023042  KaiDong Wu  Tutor: QuZhi Huang

[Abstract] To address the tendency toward static pacing in late-stage matches of conventional multiplayer first-person shooter games, a multiplayer online game titled "The Realm of Resonance" was designed and implemented based on Unity 2022.3.62f3c1, the Universal Render Pipeline, and the Photon PUN 2 networking framework. The system comprises five core modules: centralized lifecycle scheduling with Addressables configuration, hybrid Photon synchronization, first-person movement and eight-weapon combat, a dynamic resonance environment system, and NavMesh-driven AI bots. By introducing `UpdateManager` and `ObjectRecycler` object pooling, runtime overhead and garbage collection spikes are effectively suppressed. Character transforms and discrete combat events are synchronized via `IPunObservable` interpolation, RPCs, and room custom properties, while the Master Client authoritatively updates the global resonance value to trigger five environmental responses: dynamic gravity modulation, ground fire zones, neutral third-faction AI spawning, supply drops, and meteorite strikes. Full-flow tests launched from the main menu into a live Photon Cloud room confirm accurate state synchronization across all resonance triggers, sustaining an average frame rate of 67.7 FPS with zero Gen0 garbage collection allocations under concurrent AI combat and 46 active fire zones, demonstrating the stability and engineering feasibility of the system.

[Keywords] Unity; First-Person Shooter; Photon PUN; Universal Render Pipeline; Resonance Mechanism
