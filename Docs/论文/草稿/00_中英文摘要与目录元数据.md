## 中文摘要

多人联机第一人称射击游戏进入中后期后，地图交火点位往往趋于固化，战局节奏变化有限。围绕这一问题，基于 Unity 2022.3.62f3c1、通用渲染管线 URP 与 Photon PUN 2 框架开发了联机游戏《共鸣之境》。该游戏整合集中帧调度、Addressables 配置加载、四通道网络同步、第一人称八武器战斗、共鸣环境联动以及 NavMesh 机器人对抗等模块。运行期通过 `UpdateManager` 归并高频脚本更新，利用 `ObjectRecycler` 复用弹道与特效对象，并在房主端权威结算全局共鸣值，驱动重力调制、地面火海、中立机器人增援、战区空投与陨石打击五种动态变化。从主菜单接入 Photon 云端房间实测，共鸣数值与五类环境响应在多端同步一致；开启 46 处火海并伴随机器人并发交火时，平均帧率维持在 67.7 FPS，第 0 代垃圾回收增量为 0，工程方案具备实际联机运行能力。

【关键词】Unity；第一人称射击；Photon PUN；通用渲染管线；共鸣机制

## 末页英文标题与摘要

Design and Implementation of the Multiplayer Online Game "The Realm of Resonance" Based on Unity

Concord University College Fujian Normal University
Department of Information Technology  Digital Media Technology
124232023042  KaiDong Wu  Tutor: QuZhi Huang

[Abstract] Late-stage matches in multiplayer first-person shooter games often stall around fixed choke points. Built on Unity 2022.3.62f3c1, the Universal Render Pipeline, and Photon PUN 2, The Realm of Resonance links player combat directly to changing battlefield conditions. A centralized `UpdateManager` batches script lifecycle calls, while `ObjectRecycler` pools projectiles and hit effects to avoid runtime allocations. Transform streams, remote procedure calls, and room custom properties keep player poses and match events aligned across clients. Meanwhile, the Master Client calculates a global resonance score that alters jump gravity, ignites ground fire zones, spawns neutral third-faction bots, drops supplies, and calls down meteorite strikes. In live Photon Cloud tests launched from the main menu, clients stayed synchronized across all five environmental states and sustained 67.7 FPS with zero Gen0 garbage collection spikes under concurrent AI combat and 46 active fire zones.

[Keywords] Unity; First-Person Shooter; Photon PUN; Universal Render Pipeline; Resonance Mechanism
